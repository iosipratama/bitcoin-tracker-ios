Sats Keeper is a read-only Bitcoin tracker. Adding a wallet needs only a
public Bitcoin address — no account, no sign-in, no private keys.

The free app tracks two wallets. Tapping + to add a third opens this
purchase, which is a one-time unlock for unlimited wallets. There is no
subscription.

To reach it: tap + in the top right, paste any public Bitcoin address,
name the wallet, and save. Repeat once more. Tapping + a third time
opens the purchase screen.
