Sato is a read-only ("watch-only") Bitcoin tracker. It holds no private keys, cannot send or receive funds, and has no trading, exchange or custody features. There is no account system and no sign-in, so no demo account is needed.

To try the app:
1. Tap Continue on the welcome screen, then create a wallet and give it any name.
2. Paste any public Bitcoin address to see its balance. A well-known public address with a balance you can use for testing: 1A1zP1eP5QGefi2DMPTfTL5SLmv7DivfNa
3. Pull to refresh to re-fetch the balance and price.
4. Settings lets you switch the fiat currency, hide fiat, and show amounts in satoshis.

Data sources: on-chain balances come from public Esplora block explorers (mempool.space and mirrors); the BTC price comes from CoinGecko. Both are public, unauthenticated endpoints. Wallet names and addresses are stored only on the device.

In-app purchase: Sato Plus is a one-time non-consumable that lifts the one-wallet limit. The paywall appears when adding a second wallet, and can also be reached from Settings, where Restore Purchase is available.
